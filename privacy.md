# Privacy Policy — JSON → TypeMint

**Last updated: October 8, 2026**

## Overview

JSON → TypeMint is a Chrome extension that generates TypeScript, Zod, Pydantic, Go, SQL, YAML, XML, CSV, and OpenAPI code from JSON. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data We Collect

### 1. JSON Input Data
All JSON processing happens **locally in your browser**. Your JSON data **never leaves your device**. We do not collect, store, or transmit any JSON you paste or generate.

### 2. License Verification (Email)
When you purchase the Pro version, you provide an email address. We use this email to:
- Verify your Pro license status
- Store your license in our Cloudflare KV database

**What we store:** Your email address and license status (`active`).
**How long we store it:** 365 days from the date of purchase.
**Where we store it:** Cloudflare KV (Cloudflare, Inc., USA).

### 3. No Telemetry, No Tracking
We do **not** collect:
- Browsing history
- Personal information (except email for license)
- Usage analytics
- IP addresses (except transient processing by Cloudflare)

## Third-Party Services

### Cloudflare Workers
We use Cloudflare Workers to verify license status. Cloudflare may process your IP address and email to route requests. Cloudflare's Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

### Suby (Payment Processing)
We use Suby for payment processing. Suby may collect your email, payment method, and billing information. Suby's Privacy Policy: https://suby.fi/privacy
